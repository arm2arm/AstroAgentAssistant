## Notes on Author Name Variations
These variations commonly cause lookup failures — always try multiple forms:

| Author | Possible Forms |
|--------|---------------|
| Maíz-Apellániz | `Maiz-Apellaniz`, `Maiz Apellániz`, `Maiz-Apellániz`, `Maíz Apellániz` |
| Bailer-Jones | `Bailer-Jones`, `Bailer Jones`, `Bailerjones` |
| Chiappini | `Chiappini` (no variation issues) |
| Anders | `Anders` (no variation issues) |

For consortium papers, ADS uses "Gaia Collaboration" as the primary author but the lead author appears in the bibcode and first author position. When citing, use the individual lead author (Creevey, Fouesneau, etc.), not "Gaia Collaboration."
